# Corrections

Every change to a published number or claim, newest first. A record that never posts corrections is a press release.

## 2026-09-07

Independent second-pass review of all fifteen deals against the primary documents. Everything below was wrong in an earlier version of this repository and is now fixed.

- **Mount Pleasant, Wisconsin.** Recorded as having no financial incentive, on the strength of the November 2023 announcement. The March 2023 rebate of 42 percent of incremental property taxes, capped at $5 million a year, was never withdrawn, and the district's own cash flow projects $115,000,000 in developer incentives to Microsoft through 2039. Now recorded as an incentive.
- **Prince William County, Virginia.** Square footage was 11,300,000. The Court of Appeals opinion and the floor-area caps in the proffers give 10,700,000. Also removed a $180,000,000 to $200,000,000 road figure that came from the applicant's cover letter, not from any proffer, and a 50-foot "setback" that is a landscape buffer rather than a noise term.
- **Fort Meade, Florida.** Carried the developer's 450-plus jobs figure. The county performance agreement binds 50 full-time jobs at an average wage of $106,498. Upgraded from press reporting to the executed documents.
- **Richland Parish, Louisiana.** Stated the new gas plants are "expected to run 30 to 40 years." That range describes existing regional units. The correct figure is a 30-year depreciable life. Also corrected a $22.4 million payment described as a payment in lieu of taxes; it was a sales tax payment, and the payment-in-lieu period had not begun.
- **Pima County, Arizona.** Attributed to the utility a statement that the power would not come from renewable sources. It was the developer's own executive describing the existing generation mix plus certificates. Also removed an unconfirmed second nondisclosure agreement and narrowed "supervisors said they voted without knowing an NDA existed" to the one supervisor who said it.
- **Council Bluffs, Iowa.** State incentives across three awards were added as $36.6 million; the correct sum is about $38 million. Acreage of 2,200 could not be sourced and is now blank.
- **Abilene, Texas.** Removed a $70 million to $90 million city revenue projection and a city manager quote that appear in no source. Corrected a document entry that pointed two resolutions at one URL.
- **Bessemer, Alabama.** Wage stated as about $112,500; the two published figures are $112,550 and $115,000, and they are now both shown. Construction jobs conflated two different claims. Corrected what the NAACP letter actually demanded.
- **Lancaster, Pennsylvania.** A citation for closed-loop cooling pointed at the wrong section, "magnetic bearing" chillers should read magnetic levitation, and a criticism about the group being refused a seat could not be sourced and was removed.
- **Cedar Rapids, Iowa.** The Google agreement's clawback was marked all-or-nothing; the state contract requires repayment in proportion to the shortfall. The signing date on the QTS agreement was the approval date.

Earlier the same day, two errors from the research memo that started this project were caught before publication: Florida's SB 484 does not ban nondisclosure agreements as enacted, and the Lancaster community fund is $20 million from the City, not $20.25 million from the county.
