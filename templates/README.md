# Model templates and guides

What the benchmark compares signed deals against. Links only; we do not copy the documents. Clause names are summarized from the documents themselves.

| Template | Publisher, date | What it asks for |
|---|---|---|
| [Community Benefits Agreement Template (data centers)](https://climate.law.columbia.edu/sites/climate.law.columbia.edu/files/content/NAACP%20CBA%20Data%20Center%20Template%202026.pdf) | NAACP Center for Environmental and Climate Justice, 2026 (drafted with the Sabin Center) | Benefits scaled to MW, gallons, or incentives received. Community Benefit Fund with an upfront payment plus annual CPI-indexed payments, run solely by a Community Advisory Board with majority community representation. Developer-funded independent annual audits made public. Public reporting dashboard. Escalating penalties and clawback or revocation of subsidies on non-compliance. Local hire at a placeholder 50 percent, prevailing wage, union neutrality. Annual water cap, per-1,000-gallon water fund, monthly public water reporting. Annual energy, water, fuel, and emissions disclosure. Non-cost-shifting principle for grid costs. Renewable ratchet to 100 percent. No decommissioning bond and no minimum bill appear in it. |
| [Template Community Benefits Agreement (multi-purpose)](https://climate.law.columbia.edu/sites/climate.law.columbia.edu/files/content/Sabin%20Center_CBA_Multi-purpose_Template.pdf) | Sabin Center for Climate Change Law, Columbia, November 2024 | Written for pipelines and industrial facilities, not data centers specifically. Independent third-party monitor, complaint log, quarterly CBA committee, public project website, decommissioning obligation backed by developer-maintained financial security, local hire, named fund contributions, noise limit compliance, cure and remedies, public posting of the agreement. |
| [Community Benefits Agreements Database](https://climate.law.columbia.edu/content/community-benefits-agreements-database) | Sabin Center | 80-plus agreements from 1989 to 2025, the Lancaster AI Hub CBA among them. Useful for finding executed documents. |
| [How Communities Can Get a Better Deal from Data Centers](https://insights.som.yale.edu/insights/how-communities-can-get-better-deal-from-data-centers) | Yale School of Management, Sonnenfeld, Siegel, and Henriques, September 1, 2026 | Build the accountability layer first: disclosure, published agreements, scheduled audits. Then condition any incentive on a but-for test paired with a proportional clawback. |
| [Data Center Municipal Resource Hub](https://www.njeda.gov/DataCenterTA/) and [Local Finance Notice 2026-13](https://www.nj.gov/dca/dlgs/lfns/2026/2026-13.pdf) | New Jersey EDA and Department of Community Affairs, 2026 | State technical assistance for town staff. The notice recommends developer-funded road, water, sewer, and fire improvements, a facility decommissioning plan, interconnection cost responsibility settled with the utility, annual energy and water reporting, a community advisory committee with an annual public report, property-line sound benchmarks, local hiring targets, and stated consequences for non-compliance. |
| [Data centers are coming. Here's how communities can negotiate for local benefit.](https://connecthumanity.fund/data-centers-are-coming-heres-how-communities-can-negotiate-for-local-benefit/) | Connect Humanity, March 4, 2026 | Local hire, workforce training, dark fiber along data center routes, below-market fiber leases to local ISPs, revenue sharing or a community endowment, buy-local supplier provisions. Does not address audits, clawbacks, decommissioning, or noise. |
| [Data Centers: Key Reforms for State Subsidy Legislation](https://goodjobsfirst.org/data-centers-best-reforms-for-state-subsidy-legislation/) | Good Jobs First, September 23, 2025 | Facilities must stay in operation 20 years after build-out, with a clawback of 5 percent per year for each year job levels are not maintained. Ban NDAs with public officials and code names. Require beneficial-owner disclosure. Cap sales and use tax exemptions. Annual online reporting of forgone revenue. Sunset and re-evaluation every three years. |

## How the floor was set

`schema/floor.json` takes the strictest common ask across these documents for each clause, with two additions the templates mostly skip: a decommissioning bond and a minimum bill that survives the tenant leaving. Those two come from the downside case. If efficiency gains make some of this capacity redundant, as Mark Cuban has argued it might, the terms that matter are the ones written for a bust, not a boom.
